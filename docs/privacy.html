Paper Tanks — Privacy Policy

Developer: Russell Thomas
Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Paper Tanks is a standalone 1980s pencil-flick arcade game built with zero advertisements, zero third-party analytics SDKs, and zero user tracking.

1. Data Collection & Sharing

Paper Tanks does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, location data, device identifiers, contacts, or crash telemetry.

2. Local Save Data

Campaign progress, best period grades, audio settings, aiming preferences, and selected paper/decal themes are stored strictly on your device using local storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

3. Optional External Links

If you choose to open an optional external link (such as the developer's Buy Me a Coffee page or project website), that external website is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Paper Tanks collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out via the Paper Tanks GitHub repository.
