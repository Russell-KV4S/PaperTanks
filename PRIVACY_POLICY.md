# Privacy Policy for Paper Tanks

**Last Updated:** November 14, 2026

Paper Tanks is a standalone arcade game developed with zero advertisements, zero third-party analytics SDKs, and zero user tracking.

## Data Collection & Storage
- **Personal Data:** Paper Tanks does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal information, location data, device identifiers, or crash telemetry.
- **Local Save Data:** Campaign progress, best period grades, audio preferences, aiming style, and selected paper/decal themes are stored strictly on your device using local browser/app storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device.
- **Optional External Links:** If you choose to visit the optional developer support link (such as Buy Me a Coffee or itch.io), that external site operates under its own privacy policy.

## Children's Privacy
Paper Tanks does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the developer's website or itch.io project page.
